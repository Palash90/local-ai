Score DSL. A score has THREE axes: GENRE (sound world), song FORM (@section), and instrument LANES ([ROLE instr]); plus TEXTURE (how many voices play together over time).

DIRECTIVES (whole-score, one per line, before lanes):
  @tempo <bpm>     20-300 (default 120)
  @genre <name>    see GENRES below; sets scale/instruments/groove feel
  @mood <name>     joyful|moody|inspiring|dreamy|tense|epic|calm|playful
  @section <name> bars=N energy=0.0-1.0 [repeat=N]
                   song form on a shared bar grid that tiles from bar 1 across ALL lanes; energy auto-scales velocity+expression so a chorus lifts the band without editing notes. Use intro/verse/prechorus/chorus/bridge/outro. LOOP SEMANTICS: if a lane's written material is shorter than the section grid it is automatically repeated (bar-aligned) across the whole form — write your best 2-4 bar vamp and let the sections loop it with their energy lifts; the piece lasts as long as the grid says.
  '#' starts a comment.

GENRES (authentic palette + groove; feel free to fuse):
  jazz pop rock edm hiphop rnb funk blues japanese chinese indian_classical bollywood arabic korean latin bossa ambient cinematic. e.g. japanese=hirajoshi/yo pentatonic, koto/shamisen/shakuhachi, sparse taiko; indian_classical=raga (yaman/bhairav/malkauns), sitar/sarangi/shanai + tanpura drone + tabla; arabic=maqam (hijaz/kurd), santoor/oud + darbuka; jazz=dorian/mixo, 7ths/9ths, brush-swing; edm=four-on-floor synth.
  CROSS-GENRE: you may set one lane's INSTRUMENT (or scale) from a different genre for fusion (koto over jazz, sitar in cinematic, etc.).

LANES: '[ROLE INSTRUMENT vol=NN]' header then note tokens. ROLE any name (MELODY, HARMONY, BASS, RHYTHM/PERC). Any lane may be a melody — PIANO/EPIANO can carry the lead, and a low-register PIANO can be the bass (write low octaves); instrument role is set by register, not by name. INSTRUMENT alias includes world voices: PIANO EPIANO ORGAN CELESTA MUSICBOX GUITAR NYLON EGUITAR BASS EBASS SLAP FRETLESS VIOLIN VIOLA CELLO CONTRABASS STRINGS SYNTHSTRINGS CHOIR FLUTE OBOE CLARINET SAX TRUMPET TROMBONE FRENCHHORN HARP PAD SYNTH LEAD | SITAR VEENA SAROD TAMBRA EKTARA BANJO UKULELE SHAMISEN KOTO GUZHENG YANGQIN SANTOOR DULCIMER KALIMBA MBIRA SHAKUHACHI DIZI OCARINA PANFLUTE BAGPIPE FIDDL SHANAI SHEHNAI SARANGI STEELDRUM, or prog=NN (GM). vol=NN lane mix 0-100 (good defaults). Percussion lane = role RHYTHM/DRUMS/PERC* (channel 9). MELODY = single notes (E4 q); sustained chord voicings like C3:maj7 w belong in a HARMONY lane, not the melody. TEXTURE: to double/harmonise or do call-and-response, emit MULTIPLE same-role lanes (MELODY + MELODY2, HARMONY + PAD, BASS + BASS2, RHYTHM + PERC2); give a voice the full bar of rests 'R w |' on bars where it is silent, so lanes stay bar-aligned. MINIMUM TEXTURE: a full piece needs AT LEAST a MELODY lane AND a HARMONY lane (chord voicings, e.g. [HARMONY epiano vol=72] with C3:maj7/G3:7 tokens — never melody alone). Add BASS + RHYTHM lanes whenever the user mentions rhythm, groove, beat or a full band; a SINGLE lane is valid ONLY for an explicit solo request ('just piano', 'musicbox solo'). Max 8 lanes.

EVENTS (space-separated; '|' = bar line). Each lane plays ONE voice at a time (a chord is one token). Fill EVERY 4-beat bar exactly (w=4 h=2 q=1 e=.5 s=.25, '.' dots), else the piece desyncs. PITCH + DUR [+ DYN]:
  note:  C4 q, F#5 e, Bb3 h, A0 w (scientific pitch)
  chord: C:maj A3:min G:7 D:maj7 E:min7 A:sus4 B:dim (opt octave, opt 7th)
  rest:  R q   (or R w for a silent bar)
  drum:  BD KC SN RIM CLAP HH OH PEDAL CR CHIN RD RIDE LT MT HT LFT HFT CONG BONG TBL CAB MAR CLV COWB TAM TRIG + dur (kick snare rimshot clap hats cymbals toms congas timbales cabasa maracas claves cowbell tambourine triangle)
  dynamic suffix on duration (or '!' on the pitch): ! accent, f forte, m mezzo, p soft -> C4 q! / C4! q / E4ep

MUSIC THEORY (write music, not random notes):
  - Melody dances on the harmony: chord tones on strong beats (favour the 3rd/7th), stepwise passing tones on weak beats, neighbour tones, and suspensions that resolve by step across chord changes.
  - State a short MOTIF and DEVELOP it (repeat, transpose, sequence); don't invent new notes per bar.
  - Harmony: functional progressions (I-V-vi-IV; minor i-VI-III-VII) with 7th/sus colour for jazz/dreamy; for non-heptatonic genres (pentatonic/raga/maqam) prefer a tonic drone + moving melody.
  - Match mode/tempo to genre+mood (moody/tense minor 66-104; joyful/playful major 110-150; dreamy/calm 58-96 soft; inspiring rises).
  - LENGTH MATH: bars ≈ seconds × BPM ÷ 240 (four beats per bar). Make the @section bars SUM to the length the user asked for, within 20% (~60s at 84 BPM ≈ 21 bars; 30s at 120 BPM = 15 bars). After rendering, the result reports the real duration_s — if it is far from the request, fix the bar counts and render again.
  - END with a CADENCE resolving to the tonic (last two bars V->I or IV->I; final tonic 'w'; melody on the tonic) so it never cuts off abruptly. Keep pieces ~16-28 bars (~30-75s).

EXAMPLE (jazz fusion, verse->chorus, duet melody, voice-led, cadenced):
  @tempo 120
  @genre jazz
  @section verse bars=2 energy=0.5
  @section chorus bars=2 energy=1.0
  [MELODY sax vol=88]
  D4! q F4 e A4 e G4 h | D4! q C4 e D4 e R q | A4! q C5 q E5! h | D5! q C5 e D5 e E5 w |
  [MELODY2 epiano vol=80]
  R w | R w | A5! q C6 q E6! h | R e R e R h |
  [HARMONY epiano vol=72]
  D3:min7 w | G3:7 w | C4:maj7 w | G3:7 q C4:maj7 w |
  [BASS ebass vol=82]
  D2 h D2 q A2 q | G2 h G2 q D2 q | C2 w | G2 q C2 e G2 e C2 q |
  [RHYTHM]
  BD e R e HH e R e BD e R e HH e R e | BD e R e SN e R e BD e OH e SN e R e |

✗ ANTI-EXAMPLE (renders broken — never do this): one [MELODY piano] lane for a 'bossa with nice rhythm' ask (no HARMONY, no drums = thin, silent-band); chord names smuggled in as melody notes like 'Dm4 q' (invalid — the parser drops the whole bar; write 'D3:min7 q'); prose tokens like '(Verse begins)' (become bad tokens and desync the bars); a 21-bar structure that loses half its notes and ships 32s when ~60s was asked.
